    “Camp” is how many stars of a rating are explained by the reviewer's viewpoint, and so are left out of the bridged score.

      How it works

      Every rating is modelled as

      ratingij ≈ μ + ai + bj + ui·vj

      • ai: how lenient reviewer i is with everything.
      • ui·vj: agreement that depends on the reviewer's camp. Taste groups and paid review rings each form their own direction in this space.
      • bj: what's left, the part of product j's rating that every camp agrees on. The bridged rating is μ + bj.

      Intercepts are penalised more heavily than viewpoint factors, so a coordinated block of ratings is cheaper to explain as a camp than as real quality. That's the “bridging” idea behind X's Community Notes, applied to store reviews. Accounts with fewer than 3 reviews don't count, because they have no history that would show which camp they belong to.
